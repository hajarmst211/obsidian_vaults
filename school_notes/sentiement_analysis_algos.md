### 2. Classical Machine Learning (Supervised)

These techniques convert text into numerical features using methods like Bag-of-Words (BoW) or Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF). A classification algorithm is then trained on labeled data to predict the sentiment.

- **Key Algorithms:** Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Logistic Regression.
    
- **Best For:** Moderate-sized datasets where computational resources are limited.
    
- **Sources & Research:**
    
    - **Book:** Speech and Language Processing (3rd Edition Draft) by Dan Jurafsky and James H. Martin. Chapter 4 covers Naive Bayes and Sentiment Classification in detail.
        
    - **Paper:** Baselines and Bigrams: Simple, Good Sentiment and Topic Classification (Wang & Manning, 2012). This paper discusses how simple Naive Bayes and SVM baselines can perform competitively against more complex models
